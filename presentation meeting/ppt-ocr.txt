### Slide 6

**Title**: Demonstrating Your PoC

**Subtitle**: A focused demo builds credibility — show what works, why it matters, and how it scales.

**Section 1: Feature Spotlight**
*   Highlight 2-3 key capabilities
*   Walk through a live or recorded user flow
*   Focus on moments that make the audience say "that solves it"

**Section 2: Architecture Snapshot**
*   One clean diagram — Input → Process → Output
*   Avoid deep technical layers — show the flow, not the plumbing
*   Label components in plain language your audience understands

**Section 3: Business Value**
*   Tie each feature to a stakeholder benefit
*   Frame in business terms: speed, accuracy, cost, experience
*   Make the ROI link explicit — never assume the value is obvious

**Real-World Example: Apple iPhone Reveal — Steve Jobs, 2007**
Jobs didn't list specs — he demoed a call, web browsing, and music playback. Each feature tied immediately to a user moment, the "so what" was built into every gesture.

**Demo Tip**: Narrate the "so what" at every step — never assume the value is obvious to the room.

### Slide 9

**Title**: Confident Delivery & Handling Q&A

**Subtitle**: Confidence is not the absence of nerves — it is preparation meeting presence.

**Section 1: Before**
*   Know your **first 60 seconds cold** — a strong opening anchors credibility.
*   Prepare your story arc: Problem → Solution → Impact
*   Rehearse the **3 hardest questions** you might face

**Section 2: During**
*   Speak to the **outcome, not the slide** — the deck supports, not scripts.
*   Read the room — **adapt pace and depth** to engagement signals.
*   Always narrate the "**so what**" — never assume value is obvious.

**Section 3: Q&A**
*   **Pause, acknowledge, answer** — show you heard the question.
*   "**That's a great point; here's how we addressed it...**"
*   "**Let me follow up with data**" is a strength, not a weakness.

**Real-World Example: Elon Musk — Tesla Model 3 Unveil**
2016 launch event - Tough production timeline question
"That's the **hardest part**, and here's what we're doing about it" — turning uncertainty into a **trust moment**.
**Lesson**: Honestly handled well is **credibility earned**. Acknowledging limits builds more trust than a polished deflection.

**Tip**: Practice the 3 questions you fear most — when they arrive, they become your strongest moments.
